Jimbo: The AI GymBro

An adaptable trainer in your pocket. Jimbo builds personalized daily workouts designed around your schedule, equipment, and goals.

Free early access. Help shape the app before official launch.

What's Wrong With Other Apps?

Most AI trainers treat you like a robot with a generic workout schedule. If you miss a day, feel exhausted, or want to change things up, the app leaves you stuck.

Jimbo starts with a quick check-in each day. Whether you're sore from yesterday, skipped a session, or squeezed in some extra work, Jimbo automatically builds a new workout tailored for today. It makes it easy to pick up right where you left off. No guilt, just progress.

Jimbo's Features

  • Daily Workout Routines

    Complete workouts tailored to your goals. Every session maps out target muscles, warm-ups, time estimates, and exact sets, reps, and weights.

  • Daily Check-In & Adaptive Logic

    Daily check-ins keep training realistic. Tell Jimbo your soreness level, energy, and schedule changes, and your plan adapts on the spot.

  • Equipment and Location Swapping

    Train anywhere with zero hassle. Get instant options for either full gym or bodyweight setups with one-tap exercise swaps to fit whatever gear is in front of you.

  • Flexible Training Types

    Built for your training style. Whether you focus on heavy lifting, sports-specific conditioning, or mobility, Jimbo shapes the program to match.

  • Exercise Demonstration Pairings

    Exercise with the proper form every time. Access quick demonstration demos for any movement.

  • Visual Workout Calendar

    Track your consistency at a glance. Visual weekly and monthly calendars show goals, upcoming training days, rest days, and past completed training days.

  • Custom Workout Logging

    Credit for the work you put in when no one is looking. Log extra cardio, pickup games, or bonus sets and exercises so Jimbo can factor it in for the next day's plan.
